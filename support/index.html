Common questions

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your iPhone. Verba has no accounts and no servers — see the Privacy Policy. Your journal is included in your normal iPhone backup.

How do the reminders work?

Three gentle local notifications a day (you choose the times in Settings), a short note each Sunday, and one quiet note if you've been away a while. All scheduled on-device.

How do I restore Verba Pro?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed into the same Apple Account that bought it.

How do I mark an intention as come true?

Open the Practice tab → the "Your intention" card → "It came true." The option appears once an intention is at least three days old.
